# Desert Rose photos

Photographs from the Desert Rose Workation, used by `../index.html`.

Upload originals straight from the camera or phone — no need to resize or
rename first. They get cropped, compressed and renamed before they are placed
on the page.

Sizes the page uses:

- `desert-rose-hero.jpg` — the banner behind the title, 1800px wide
- gallery frames — 1600px on the long edge
- inline photo breaks — 1800px wide

Keep each file under roughly 250KB so the page stays quick to load.

## Two frames that came from the news covers

`dr-lodge-evening.jpg` and `dr-journal-spread.jpg` are the 2025 edition, taken
from `../../news-images/`: they are the only frames we hold that show the whole
gathering, mixed cohort and all. Both are smaller than the sizes above —
800px and 1280px on the long edge — because that is the size we have. They
hold up at the tile sizes they are placed in; replace them with the camera
originals if those turn up. `dr-lodge-evening.jpg` is also the cover of two
entries in the story list on the same page.
