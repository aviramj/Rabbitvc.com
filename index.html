Our Team

Aviram Jenik

General Partner

Aviram Jenik, an entrepreneur, investor, and advisor in the B2B sector, co-founded and led Beyond Security as its CEO until its acquisition by Fortra in 2021. With a track record of success, he previously co-founded two additional startups, both achieving fruitful exits. As an astute angel investor, Mr. Jenik has amassed a portfolio of over 40 companies, predominantly in the B2B SaaS domain across Israel, the US, and Korea. Focusing primarily on early-stage investments, he frequently adopts an active, hands-on approach, assisting portfolio startups in refining their go-to-market strategies and facilitating their initial forays into the US market.

MJ Kang

General Partner

MJ brings a strong growth track record of building category leaders through transformative and global scaling phases, including at Apple and Tesla. She has held CXO-level roles with U.S. startups across EdTech and AI and led global expansion initiatives for Asian technology leaders. MJ is a trusted partner to founders, boards, and investors, aligning strategy and execution to accelerate global growth and maximize investor returns. Her global founder-investor network enables exceptional deal flow and strategic partnership access across the U.S. and Asia. She has deep domain expertise across consumer electronics, EV, consumer DTC, and AI, as well as functional expertise in go-to-market strategy, experiential marketing, partner development, investor relations, and public affairs.

SK (Soo Keun) Lee

COO

SK has been the CEO of Jiran Group Holdings since 2016. Prior, SK was Namo Editor, Inc’s CEO. Prior, SK was a long time Microsoft executive in the APAC region, holding positions such as Microsoft Xbox Channel Operation Manager, Windows and Office Marketing Team Chief Manager, Pricing and Licensing Manager, APOC (Asia Pacific Operation Center) Project Manager, and FPP (Full Packaged Products) Supply Chain Manager. He also held a position at Hyundai Marine and Insurance. SK has a Bachelor’s degree in International Commerce from University of ChungAng in Seoul, Korea, and a Master of Business Administration from University of SoGang, Seoul, Korea.

Sun Cho

Growth Partner

Sun Cho is an accomplished leader in the gaming industry, with a proven track record in both Korea and the US. She is an advisor and previously held the position of General Manager for Korea at now.gg. Sun was the CEO and Founder of Kiyat Games, a Silicon Valley-based mobile game publisher focused on bringing high-quality Korean games to the global market. Sun’s career journey also includes serving as a Business Development Director at AERIA GAMES & ENTERTAINMENT, a Marketing Manager at Onnet USA, and roles as a Product Manager and Brand Manager at NEOWIZ. She has also held positions as a Project Manager at Jaemi & Changjo and as a Marketer at BattleTop. Sun has a B.S. in Psychology from CHUNG-ANG UNIVERSITY in Seoul, Korea. She holds certifications as a Brand Specialist and as a Korean Traditional Liquor Sommelier.
