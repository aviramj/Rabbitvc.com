Mission — help you go global
Our thesis

Korean talent.
Global products.

Korea has world-class engineering, design, and product talent. What it doesn’t always have is a clear playbook for selling globally from Day One. That’s where we come in.

We focus on B2B SaaS, cybersecurity, and adjacent infrastructure software, where small, opinionated teams can outrun much larger competitors with the right wedge into the US market.

  • Pre-seed & seed checks of $50K–$200K, sweet spot $100K.
  • Hands-on US go-to-market and customer introductions.
  • An operator network across Seoul, San Francisco, and Tel Aviv.
