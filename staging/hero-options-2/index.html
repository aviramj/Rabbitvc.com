How this round differs from the first

The first four were all one idea — a big statement on navy — and three of them leaned on the rabbit blown up to half the screen. Here the mark stays at nav size in every direction, and the five spread deliberately across light and dark, image and no image, statement-first and content-first.

Still true from round one

Colours are sampled from the deck, the cards and the banners — navy #0A0E27, blue #3B8EFF, indigo #2E2A6E, cream #F5F4EE, gold #B4924F, ink #1B1B3A. The illustrated cityscape is gone in all five, and the site's teal and orange still need retiring alongside whichever direction wins.

Live content

Index, Ticker and Broadsheet pull real names, descriptions and headlines already on the site, so they stay current as the portfolio and the news page grow. Meridian's clocks are the actual local times in the three offices.
