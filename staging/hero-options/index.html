Where the colours come from

Every option uses only colours already in the identity — sampled from the Fund II deck, the business cards and the banners. Nothing new was invented.

  • #0A0E27Deck navy
  • #2E2A6EBanner indigo
  • #3B8EFFDeck blue
  • #F5F4EEPaper / cream
  • #B4924FCard gold
  • #1B1B3ACard ink

What changed

  • The illustrated cityscape photo is gone in all four. Nothing is placed behind the type except flat brand colour and, where it helps, the rabbit line-mark from the logo.
  • Headline wording follows the deck cover — “Beyond Capital.” as the statement, “Global Reach · Rapid Scale · Day One” as the supporting line, rather than one long run-on sentence.
  • Buttons are square-edged and thin-bordered rather than rounded and glowing, which reads more institutional.

One thing to decide alongside this

The rest of the site currently runs on teal #0bb4aa and orange #ff8a5b, which appear nowhere in the deck, the cards or the banners. Whichever hero is chosen, the site palette should move to the navy / blue / cream set above so the homepage, the deck and the cards are recognisably the same firm.
