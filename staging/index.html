Our Team

Aviram Jenik

General Partner

Aviram Jenik, an entrepreneur, investor, and advisor in the B2B sector, co-founded and led Beyond Security as its CEO until its acquisition by Fortra in 2021. With a track record of success, he previously co-founded two additional startups, both achieving fruitful exits. As an astute angel investor, Mr. Jenik has amassed a portfolio of over 40 companies, predominantly in the B2B SaaS domain across Israel, the US, and Korea. Focusing primarily on early-stage investments, he frequently adopts an active, hands-on approach, assisting portfolio startups in refining their go-to-market strategies and facilitating their initial forays into the US market.

MJ Kang

General Partner

MJ brings a strong growth track record of building category leaders through transformative and global scaling phases, including at Apple and Tesla. She has held CXO-level roles with U.S. startups across EdTech and AI and led global expansion initiatives for Asian technology leaders. MJ is a trusted partner to founders, boards, and investors, aligning strategy and execution to accelerate global growth and maximize investor returns. Her global founder-investor network enables exceptional deal flow and strategic partnership access across the U.S. and Asia. She has deep domain expertise across consumer electronics, EV, consumer DTC, and AI, as well as functional expertise in go-to-market strategy, experiential marketing, partner development, investor relations, and public affairs.

SK (Soo Keun) Lee

COO

SK is a seasoned veteran in cross border business operations with a wealth of experience in the global tech industry. At Rabbit, SK oversees finance and operations. He has served as the CEO of Jiran Group USA since 2016 and was previously the VP of Namo Interactive. Prior to these roles, SK spent over a decade as an executive at Microsoft in the APAC region. His leadership there spanned several key functions, including Chief Manager of the Windows and Office Marketing teams, Xbox Channel Operations, FPP Supply Chain, and APAC Project Manager for Pricing and Licensing.

Sun Cho

Growth Partner

Sun Cho is an accomplished leader in the gaming industry with a proven track record of success in both Korea and the U.S. At Rabbit, she supports operations and maintains close communication with portfolio firms to identify and execute growth opportunities. Sun also serves as an advisor and previously held the position of General Manager for Korea at now.gg. As the CEO and Founder of Kiyat Games, a Silicon Valley based mobile game publisher, she focused on bringing high quality Korean titles to the global market. Her extensive career also includes roles as Business Development Director at Aeria Games & Entertainment and Product & Brand Manager at Neowiz.
