The staged homepage at 1440px. The ticker runs; hovering it pauses.

What it is made of

Three pieces, each from something the partners already approved.

From round two · 03

The treatment

Navy ground, one indigo light source, and the eighteen portfolio companies moving slowly along the bottom in two rows. The motion is the only animation on the page.

The firm's motto, verbatim

The motto

“Beyond Capital: Global Reach. Rapid Scale. Day One.”, set across three lines exactly as the homepage breaks it today, and kept in white, with the blue held back for the eyebrow, the rule and the button.

From round two · 04

The Latest column

The four most recent items from the news page, on the right, behind a hairline rule. It shows the firm is active without anyone having to say so.

It maintains itself

Nothing in the hero has to be updated by hand.

  • TickerReads the names straight out of the portfolio section further down the same page. Adding a company adds it to the hero.
  • LatestFetches the four newest items from the news page on load. If that ever fails, the four shipped in the HTML stay put rather than leaving a gap.
  • MotionPauses on hover, and stops entirely for anyone whose system asks for reduced motion.
  • NavKeeps the hero's colours for as long as the hero is behind it, so the bar never turns pale over navy in light mode.

Set in three lines. The motto breaks after the colon and after “Global Reach.”, the same way the current homepage breaks it, so no phrase is ever split across a line. The parts are held together, and only the break between “Rapid Scale.” and “Day One.” is allowed to move. Laptops are wide but short, so below 820px of viewport height the hero tightens and Latest shows three items instead of four, which keeps the ticker above the fold rather than clipping it.

The background question

The navy is close to the site's black, but not the same one.

Both are near-black, so at a glance they pass. Side by side they are two different blacks: the hero is bluer and roughly half again as saturated, so where the ticker ends and the page begins there is a faint horizontal band, and it reads as two pages meeting rather than one page continuing.

#0A0E27H 232° · S 59% · L 9.6%The hero. Straight off the Fund II deck.
#0E151FH 215° · S 38% · L 8.8%The site's dark ground, everywhere below the hero.

The fix is one token. Point the site's --bg at the deck navy and the seam disappears, because the whole page is then sitting on the colour the deck already uses. Its two lighter greys should follow the same hue so cards and panels stay in family.

In light mode the navy is fine as it is. A dark hero over a light page reads as deliberate, and the nav already keeps the hero's colouring while the hero is behind it, so there is no pale bar over navy.

The background is the smaller problem. The accents are the real mismatch: the hero's blue is #3B8EFF at 215°, while everything below still runs on teal #0BB4AA at 176° and orange #FF8A5B at 17°. Scroll one screen from a blue button and the buttons, links and card hovers turn teal. Neither teal nor orange appears in the deck, the cards or the banners.

Open questions for the partners

Four things worth a decision before this goes live.

  1. Swap the palette. One token moves the page ground to the deck navy and closes the seam; the teal and orange accents move to the deck blue. This is the change that makes the hero look like it belongs to the site rather than sitting on top of it.
  2. Two ticker rows or one? Two carries all eighteen names at a readable size; one is calmer. Easy to change either way.
  3. The kicker reads “Seed · Cross-border · Deep tech.” Confirm that is how the partners want the fund described in the first line a visitor reads.
  4. On a phone the ticker falls below the fold and Latest shows three items rather than four. Worth confirming that ordering is right for mobile visitors.

Where it lives

Staged on a branch. The live homepage has not been touched.

The built page is staging/index.html with its own staging/hero.css, on the branch claude/homepage-professional-redesign-3cpj48. It loads the site's real stylesheet on top of that, so everything below the hero is the current homepage. The staging copy was rebuilt from the live page on 8 September, picking up the nav reorder, the removal of the duplicate Contact Us, and the site-wide copy corrections.

Nothing was added to the shared stylesheet, so the live homepage renders exactly as it does today until the partners say otherwise.
